Classes


What does this cost? Since most conventions are out-of state (we are based in Indiana), we kindly ask that travel and lodging costs be covered. For event attendees, classes are completely free. Please contact us directly to discuss the particulars.

Can we invite you to apply as a vendor and still have you do the classes? No. Sorry, but we have no interest in being a vendor. The market for custom calligraphy is too small for us to justify paying vendor tabling fees in addition to time and travel costs. It is not financially realistic and we only have one staff member.

What can I expect at a class? We can teach a maximum of 15 students at a time. Classes run for thirty minutes, or can be extended up to one hour. We provide clipboards, paper, instructions, and erasable gel pens. Our instructors are in full costume. After a brief introduction, students will be given reference materials and can raise their hand to get an instructor's help. Five minutes before the end of the event, the instructor will notify the participants of the time, and in the final minute will make closing announcements. Participants can take home their paper for free. Note: wooden sticks, string and seals are not provided, as it is easier for participants to take home just the paper. We can run a maximum of 4 classes per day.
